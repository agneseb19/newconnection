# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS (no build step), kept because the owner previews with VS Code Go Live and uploads the folder as-is. Three.js r160 and GSAP are vendored in `js/vendor`.

## Users

- Primary: someone with an urgent fault (cracked screen, phone that will not charge, battery dying), usually searching from their phone for a repairer nearby, right now.
- Local people from Valmontone and nearby towns who want someone they can trust before handing over their device.

## Product Purpose

New Connection is a shop in Valmontone that repairs and sells smartphones, computers, PS5 and TVs. The site must get a worried visitor to the counter (or on the phone) quickly, and make them feel the device is in good hands. Success: the visitor calls, sends a request, or walks in.

## Positioning

- Personal relationship: Gianmarco follows each customer in person and explains what is happening in plain words.
- Fast repairs: many jobs (screens, batteries) done quickly.

## Operating Context

Physical shop at Via Molino San Giovanni 37, 00038 Valmontone (RM). Open Monday–Saturday 9–13 and 16–19, closed Sunday. Customers bring the device to the counter; diagnosis, then repair.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Repairs: screen, battery, charging port, cameras, and more. Sales: smartphones, desktop and laptop PCs, PS5, TV.
- Open decisions: phone, WhatsApp and email are not yet provided (`js/config.js`); the request form needs one of them to send. No prices, turnaround times or warranties have been stated: do not invent them.

## Brand Commitments

- Logo "NEW CONNECTION" with its blue→violet colours stays (`assets/logo-dark.png`, `assets/logo-light.png`). The rest of the visual world may change.
- Keep the scroll-driven exploded iPhone on the home page and the 3D product explorer (iPhone, MacBook, PS5, TV).

## Evidence on Hand

- Photo of Gianmarco (currently linked from flashrepairstore.it).
- 3D models: iPhone 15 Pro Max (polyman, CC BY 4.0), MacBook (pmndrs, MIT); PS5 and TV built in code.
- No real Google reviews yet, no opening promotions yet: never fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Urgency first: a visitor with a broken device finds how to reach the shop within seconds, on a phone.
2. A person, not a chain: show who repairs your device.
3. Plain words: describe faults the way customers say them, not technical jargon.
4. Local and concrete: the street, the hours, the town.
